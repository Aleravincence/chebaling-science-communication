# Chebaling House Style

This is the measured house style from the completed 2026 Chebaling human-wildlife-conflict article. Use it as a starting point, not as a reason to force unrelated content into the same page count.

## Page system

- Page size: A4, 21.0 x 29.7 cm.
- Cover section: all margins 0 cm; header and footer distance 1.25 cm; clean cover with no running header or page number.
- Body section: top and bottom margins 2.54 cm; left margin 3.0 cm; right margin 2.5 cm; header and footer distance 1.25 cm.
- Page background: `#F4F1E9`.
- Callout and metadata panel background: `#EAF3F1`.
- Body copy is left-aligned or justified; never center long body paragraphs.

## Cover typography

- English kicker: 9 pt, `#FF6A3B`, Arial/黑体, wide letter spacing created with spaces; 25 pt after.
- Cover title: 40 pt, bold, Arial/黑体, `#15857A`, exact 46 pt line spacing, 5 pt after each line.
- Cover subtitle or standfirst: 12 pt, Arial/微软雅黑, `#606060`, 40 pt after.
- Cover metadata: 12 pt, Arial/微软雅黑, `#707070`, 4 pt after each line.
- Footer line: 8 pt, Arial/微软雅黑, `#A0A0A0`.
- The cover title may be split across 2–3 short lines. Keep the subject and outcome visible without a decorative rule under the title.

## Body hierarchy

- Body text: 12 pt (小四), 微软雅黑, `#2F3B34`, justified, 1.3 line spacing, 4 pt after, first-line indent 0.74 cm (two Chinese characters).
- Section heading, level 1: 15 pt, 黑体, bold, `#15857A`, 1.3 line spacing, 18 pt before and 8 pt after. Use for numbered sections and appendices.
- Section heading, level 2: 14 pt, 微软雅黑, bold, `#2F3B34`, 1.3 line spacing, 12 pt before and 4 pt after, no first-line indent.
- Pull quote or final emphasis: 12 pt bold, primary teal, preserve body line spacing.
- Do not use all caps for Chinese headings. Keep heading numbering stable when sections are inserted or removed.

## Captions, notes, tables, and references

- Figure caption: 10 pt, 微软雅黑, `#8A8A8A`, centered, 10 pt after. Put figure number, title, data year, scope, and source in the caption or immediately below it.
- Table caption: 10 pt, 微软雅黑, `#8A8A8A`, centered, 10 pt after.
- Table note: 10 pt, 微软雅黑, `#8A8A8A`, centered, 10 pt after.
- Table body: 8.5 pt, 微软雅黑, table grid, 1 pt before and after in cells, vertically centered. Header row uses `#D9EAD3`; bold or semi-bold the header.
- Reference entries: 8.5 pt, hanging indent 0.64 cm, 2 pt after. Keep one DOI or URL per entry.
- Header: 9 pt, 微软雅黑, `#8A8A8A`, centered.
- Footer page number: 9 pt, `#8A8A8A`, centered.
- Hyperlinks: `#0563C1`, single underline. Use true Word hyperlinks, not colored text alone.

## In-flow figures

- Insert infographics centered at 6.25 in (15.88 cm) wide.
- Keep the aspect ratio intact. A 3:2 image is usually about 6.25 x 4.17 in.
- Put the caption after the image, not inside the infographic.
- Figure captions must identify the source marker and distinguish measured data from a plan or illustrative scenario.
- Avoid placing a figure directly under a heading if it creates an orphaned heading at the page bottom.

## Infographic system

- Canvas: 1800 px wide; 1200 or 1320 px high; export at 300 dpi.
- Background: `#F4EFE1`.
- Cards: `#FFFEF9`, border `#DDD7C8`, corner radius 17–28 px.
- Title: 44–48 px bold, dark ink; subtitle 24–28 px muted.
- Card titles: 25–34 px bold in the category colour; body text 18–24 px; source note 18–20 px.
- Use 3–5 cards per figure. Keep one visual idea per row.
- Use colour semantically: green for protection or evidence, gold for warning or value, brown for risk or governance, blue for planning or participation, sage for monitoring.
- Every infographic should state its source and whether it shows data, a plan, or a conceptual pathway.
- Check text at final print size, not only in the source canvas.

## Pagination and proofing

- Do not solve crowding by reducing captions below 9 pt or body text below 10.5 pt.
- Prefer a deliberate page break before a large table or infographic over splitting a small caption or table row across pages.
- Inspect every page at full size after Word-to-PDF export. Then inspect a contact sheet for rhythm and blank pages.
- Common defects: clipped captions, duplicated citation markers, orphaned headings, old titles in headers, figures with text below 7 pt at print size, and tables with one stranded row.
